Our Story

Welcome to CakeHaus

◆

Where every cake tells a story

CakeHaus is a home-based bakery in Raslouw, Pretoria, specialising in handcrafted cakes and cupcakes made with love and premium ingredients. From our signature Coconut Almond Glaze cakes drizzled with Milkybar, Bar One, or Lotus, to beautifully piped buttercream cupcakes and bespoke celebration cakes — every order is made fresh, just for you.

Founded by Farah Khan, CakeHaus brings together elegant design and irresistible flavour. Whether you're celebrating a birthday, a milestone, or simply treating yourself, we're here to make it sweeter.
